Research

The questions I keep coming back to: bodies, brains, and what models actually see.

Northwestern University | SPICE Lab | Advisor: Prof. Karan Ahuja | Aug. 2026 - Present

Graduate Research Assistant - Human Motion from Wearable Sensors

How much can six small motion sensors (IMUs) tell us about a whole body's pose? I study how far more data takes a pose model, and where it stops helping.

  • Built a unified pipeline that turns ~1,490 hours of human motion from 8 datasets (optical mocap, Xsens, multi-view video) into SMPL bodies and simulated 6-IMU signals, with person-level train/test splits to prevent leakage.
  • Ran a 60-run data-scaling study of the DynaIP pose model (5 data sizes x 4 dataset groups x 3 seeds); training on combined data cut simulated error to 3.5 cm MPJPE vs. 6.2-7.2 cm for the best single-dataset models.
  • Found that the sim-to-real gap is the real bottleneck: error on real IMUs stayed at 10-14 cm regardless of data size, traced to high-frequency sensor energy that simulation misses (8-40% vs. 0.4-3% above 5 Hz).
Python, PyTorch, Slurm
Northwestern University | with Rahul Islam & Prof. Karan Ahuja | 2026

Synchrony Is Not Readability - Evaluating Lip-Sync Metrics

A lip-sync video can score well on the standard metric and still be impossible to lip-read. We tested whether the metric everyone reports actually measures what matters.

  • Showed that SyncNet confidence (LSE-C), the standard lip-sync metric, does not predict whether translated speech is readable from the lips.
  • Benchmarked 6 lip-sync generators on translated visual speech in English and Mandarin.
  • Second author on the paper, currently under review at ACL Rolling Review.
OpenReview
Huazhong Agricultural University | Mentor: Prof. Yan Tong | Wuhan, China (Hybrid) | Aug. 2022 - May 2025

Kiwifruit Quality Grading - Computer Vision & Machine Learning

Can a camera grade fruit as reliably as a trained eye? I built the hand-crafted feature baseline that the deep model had to beat.

  • Engineered a 53-dimensional feature set (12 color, 16 texture, 12 shape, 13 defect features) from 1,701 labeled kiwifruit images across 3 quality classes.
  • Checked feature independence with Pearson correlation (< 0.5) and mutual information, and ranked features with permutation importance.
  • Benchmarked 6 classifiers with 5-fold cross-validation (best: Logistic Regression, 85.7% macro-F1), and supported validation of ResTNet, a hybrid ResNet + Vision Transformer that reached 99.5% macro-F1.
Expert Systems with Applications

Machine Learning Research Assistant - EEG & fNIRS

Can brain signals show how cognition changes after a stroke, or what someone is feeling? This is where I first fell for research, and it led to three papers.

  • Preprocessed 35GB of clinical EEG/fNIRS recordings from 32+ subjects: ICA artifact removal, power spectral density via FFT, and functional connectivity features.
  • Co-authored a study comparing healthy controls with mild, moderate, and severe post-stroke cognitive impairment, using ANOVA with permutation testing and Bonferroni correction (p < 0.001).
  • First-authored a systematic review of EEG and fNIRS for Alzheimer's assessment and therapy, and built an EEG emotion-recognition pipeline benchmarking RF, SVM, KNN, and XGBoost (sole-author IEEE paper).
